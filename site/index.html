Engineered for control, accuracy, and speed.

Kareer eliminates tedious copy-pasting across dozens of job applications without sacrificing accuracy or handing your private credentials to a third-party server.

Accurate Field Detection

Understands standard inputs, multiline textareas, radio groups, checkboxes, searchable comboboxes, date pickers, and multi-select dropdowns across diverse ATS layouts.

Truthful Grounding

Rigid prompt constraints enforce truthfulness. Kareer never invents employment dates, past companies, metrics, or certifications. Missing data is flagged for manual input.

Multi-Step Applications

Maintains session memory and application state as you progress through multi-page application flows on platforms like Ashby, Workday, and Lever.

Inline Narrative Assistance

Tailor open-ended answers (“Why do you want to work here?”) directly inside the form using your saved profile context and target company information.

Human Review & Safeguards

Intentionally halts before electronic signatures, legal attestations, background check consents, and interactive assessments so you maintain final authorization.

Local-First Architecture

Your profile, resume text, notes, and credentials stay in your browser's private extension storage. Kareer operates no remote servers and logs no telemetry.

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

Supply your own OpenRouter API key. Use high-speed, cost-effective models like Gemini 2.0 Flash or Claude 3.5 Sonnet for fractions of a cent per application.

100% Free & Open Source

Published under the MIT License. Audit the codebase, inspect network traffic, report issues, and contribute new ATS adapters in public.

Four steps to faster applications.

Get up and running in under five minutes. Kareer works alongside you in the browser, leaving you in complete control of submission.

STEP 01

Install Kareer

Install the extension for Chrome, Chromium, or Firefox, or run as a Tampermonkey userscript.

STEP 02

Add your profile

Save your contact information, resume context, links, and applicant notes locally in your browser extension storage.

STEP 03

Add your AI key

Supply your own OpenRouter API key. It lives safely inside the sandboxed extension background worker and never touches web pages.

STEP 04

Review and autofill

Open an application page, let Kareer match and fill supported fields, inspect the results, and submit with full confidence.

Your data isn't our business.

Kareer is architected with a strict local-first, zero-telemetry boundary. We do not operate a remote user database or track your browsing activity.

Local Browser Storage

Your resume text, contact details, notes, and settings are stored locally in your browser's private extension storage. Kareer operates no remote servers to store your profile.

Zero Telemetry & No Tracking

The extension contains no analytics SDKs, behavioral telemetry, error monitors, or tracking pixels. We never sell user data or operate an advertising network.

Direct AI Transmission

When you click Autofill, relevant question labels and relevant excerpts of your profile are transmitted directly from your browser to OpenRouter via HTTPS. No intermediary server inspects the payload.

Strict API Key Isolation

Your OpenRouter API key is stored in privileged extension storage and used only by the background service worker. Web pages you visit cannot access or read your API key.

Verifiable & Transparent

Every line of code handling storage, network dispatch, and DOM actuation is publicly inspectable in our GitHub repository.

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers regarding pricing, models, storage, and automation boundaries.

Is Kareer free?
Yes. Kareer is completely free and open-source under the MIT license. There are no subscriptions, paywalls, or premium tiers. Because you provide your own OpenRouter API key, you pay OpenRouter directly for model inference (typically fractions of a cent per application when using efficient models like Gemini 2.0 Flash).
Does Kareer store my resume on a Kareer server?
No. Kareer does not operate any backend servers or central databases. Your resume, profile, application notes, and API key are stored locally in your browser's private extension storage. When you trigger autofill, only the relevant form field questions and relevant profile sections are sent directly to OpenRouter via HTTPS.
Which browsers are supported?
Kareer natively supports Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, and other Chromium-based browsers, as well as Mozilla Firefox. It can also be built and run as a Tampermonkey userscript across any desktop browser.
Which job sites does Kareer support?
Kareer has dedicated adapters for Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, and Workday, handling custom selects, location typeaheads, and multi-page flows. For other job boards and career pages, Kareer includes an intelligent generic fallback engine supporting standard HTML inputs, textareas, selects, radios, and checkboxes.
Does Kareer automatically submit applications?
By default, Kareer never automatically submits an application. It scans, fills, and presents all filled fields for candidate review. An optional "Auto Submit" setting exists in Settings (default OFF) which only activates if all required fields are validated, no errors exist, and the candidate has reviewed the page. Kareer strictly halts before e-signatures, legal attestations, background checks, and assessments.
What AI model does Kareer use?
You choose the model through OpenRouter. We recommend google/gemini-2.0-flash for its high speed, strong JSON structure compliance, and low cost. You can also select models from Anthropic (e.g. Claude 3.5 Sonnet), OpenAI, or open-source weights.
Is Kareer open source?
Yes. All source code, tests, fixtures, and build configurations are openly maintained under the MIT License at github.com/Amro212/kareer.
